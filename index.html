The kind of problems I fix

I help make sense of business challenges, bring business and technical teams onto the same page, and turn the agreed direction into practical delivery. The work may involve people, process, product or technology often a combination of all four.

Customers in every market needed to go live faster

Problem
Business teams across 90+ countries needed their data on one platform quickly, while the platform team needed clear, consistent requirements from each market.
What I did
Acted as the link between the regional teams and the platform team, agreed a repeatable onboarding approach and tracked every region against a shared plan.
Outcome
Onboarding timelines cut by about half.

New releases took too long to reach the business

Problem
Features were waiting on slow manual testing, so the business waited longer for what it had asked for.
What I did
Worked with the delivery and engineering teams to build an automation framework for the repeat checks.
Outcome
Testing cycles down 60%, so releases reached the business sooner.

Business stakeholders and delivery teams lacked a shared view of progress

Problem
Sponsors and delivery teams were working from different views of progress, risk and performance.
What I did
Agreed the measures that mattered to the business with the delivery team and set up KPI tracking that everyone could see.
Outcome
One clear view of progress, risks and operational performance.
