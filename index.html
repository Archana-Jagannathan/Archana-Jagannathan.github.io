The kind of problems I fix

A lot of delivery challenges come down to unclear ownership, slow handovers or processes that rely too heavily on manual work. These are examples of the kinds of problems I have helped teams address.

Onboarding took too long, in too many countries

Problem
Data had to be brought onto one platform across 90+ countries, each with its own quirks.
What I did
Built a repeatable onboarding approach and helped every region work to the same delivery rhythm.
Outcome
Onboarding timelines reduced by around 50%.

Testing was slowing every release

Problem
Manual test cycles were the bottleneck on big data products.
What I did
Designed an automation framework so the repeat checks ran themselves.
Outcome
Testing cycle time reduced by 60%, helping teams prepare releases more efficiently.

No one could see how delivery was really going

Problem
Status lived in people's heads and in slide decks that were out of date.
What I did
Set up KPI-driven tracking that teams and sponsors both trusted.
Outcome
Clearer visibility into delivery progress, risks and operational performance.

How I usually work

  1. Understand the real problemI start by listening to the people involved and understanding where work is getting held up.
  2. Make progress visibleI agree on a small set of useful measures so teams and stakeholders can see what is moving and what needs attention.
  3. Simplify and automateWhere it makes sense, I use workflows, integrations and AI tools to reduce repetitive work.
  4. Keep people alignedClear, regular updates help teams and stakeholders understand decisions, progress and next steps.
